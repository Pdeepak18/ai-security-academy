# Shipping and delivery

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United
States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 19 USD.

International orders ship from the Rotterdam warehouse and typically take 7 to
14 business days. Customs duties are the responsibility of the recipient.

Orders placed before 2pm local time ship the same day. Tracking numbers are
emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.

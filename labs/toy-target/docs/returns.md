# Returns and refunds

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, provided the widget is unused
and in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 5 business days of the returned item being inspected.

Damaged items are replaced free of charge if reported within 48 hours of
delivery, with photographs attached to the support ticket.

Custom-configured widgets are non-refundable unless faulty. Escalations that
cannot be resolved by support are handled by the duty manager.

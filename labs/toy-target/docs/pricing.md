# Acme Widgets pricing

The Standard widget costs 42 USD. The Pro widget costs 89 USD and includes a
two-year warranty. Enterprise pricing starts at 5000 USD per year for up to 50
seats, billed annually with a 10 percent discount for prepayment.

Volume discounts apply above 200 units in a single order. Talk to the sales
team for anything above 1000 units; those deals are handled case by case.

All prices are in USD and exclude tax. Prices were last reviewed in March 2026.
